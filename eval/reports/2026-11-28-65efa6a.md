## Limitations

- No human relevance labels: every grade is an LLM's (Gemini primary; Groq and Claude Cowork for agreement and robustness). The judges share one rubric and may share biases; agreement between them is not agreement with people.
- 25 fictional profiles (about 70% India-based); confidence intervals are over these 25 profiles and do not cover other kinds of job seeker.
- One frozen snapshot of the job pool (2026-11-25); the pool changes daily.
- Rankings are the API's top 30, so recall is Recall@30 over judged relevant jobs (grade >= 2), not Recall@50.
- Latency comes from the 2026-11-24 benchmark (11,531-job pool) on the owner's laptop, not from the snapshot (23,705 searchable jobs).
- Claude Cowork's first 118 grades were made from the labelling tool's card, without a confidence; its later batches use the judge's exact input.
